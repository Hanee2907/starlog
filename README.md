# ✦ Starlog – Your days, written in the stars

**🌐 Live demo: https://hanee2907.github.io/starlog/**

Starlog is a mood journal where every day becomes a star in your own night sky.
Write a few lines about your day, pick how it felt, and a new star is born.

## ✨ How it works

- ⭐ **Every entry is a star.** Its colour shows your mood and its size shows how much you wrote.
- 🌌 **Good days draw constellations.** Two or more good days in a row get joined by lines, and each constellation gets its own name, like *The Golden Dragon*.
- 💫 **Great days sparkle**, and the whole sky twinkles, with shooting stars every now and then.
- 🔍 **Hover a star** to peek at the day. **Click it** to read, edit or delete the entry.

## 📊 Insights

- Stars this month and your current writing streak
- Mood mix bar for the month
- Your happiest weekday
- Which **#tag** shows up on your best days (write #friends, #school, #family... in your entries)

## 🧰 Features

- Browse past months
- Save your sky as a PNG image
- Sample sky for first-time visitors
- Works on phones, tablets and desktops
- Everything is saved in your browser (localStorage), so your journal stays private

## 🛠️ Built with

- HTML5
- CSS3 (glass panels, gradients, CSS animations, responsive grid)
- JavaScript (Canvas 2D animation, date logic, localStorage)
- Fonts: [Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces) and [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans)

## 🚀 Run it

Download or clone this repo and open `index.html` in your browser. You don't need to install anything.
